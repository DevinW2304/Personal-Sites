Android · AI · HCI

🌱 Plant AI

Personalized plant care · Room DB · Recommendations Case Study

Plant AI is an Android app that helps users grow healthier plants using a mix of local data and AI-generated care suggestions. I designed the interaction flows to feel calm and supportive, with clear affordances and low-friction input.

The project explores the intersection of HCI, accessibility, and everyday routines: how can we surface just enough intelligence to help, without overwhelming users with notifications or jargon?
