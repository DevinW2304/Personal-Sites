Gift Guide
Personalized recommendations · Guided input · Saved lists Case Study

Gift Guide is a Kotlin/Jetpack Compose Android app that helps people find thoughtful gift ideas— not generic lists. Users describe who they're shopping for, including interests, occasion, and budget, and the app turns that into personalized suggestions that actually feel relevant.

The focus is on keeping the experience calm and human: conversational inputs, clear hierarchy, and saved lists that support real-world planning instead of endless scrolling.
