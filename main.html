Android · Product Thinking

🌱 Plant AI

Plant care assistant · Local storage · Gentle reminders Case Study

Plant AI is an Android app that helps people keep their plants alive without having to remember every detail. It combines simple schedules with light intelligence to suggest when to water and check in, based on the plants a user cares about.

I focused on keeping the flows relaxed and easy to read: clear hierarchy, friendly copy, and interactions that feel more like a quiet assistant than a loud notification system.
